Acme Widgets Product Overview

Acme Widgets is a cloud-based inventory management platform for small retailers. It helps store owners track stock levels, reorder products automatically, and sync inventory across multiple sales channels.

Pricing starts at $10 per month for the Starter plan, which supports up to 500 products. The Pro plan costs $29 per month and supports unlimited products plus barcode scanning. Enterprise pricing is available on request for teams with more than ten warehouse locations.

Acme Widgets integrates with Shopify, WooCommerce, and Square out of the box. Setup usually takes less than fifteen minutes, and no credit card is required for the 14-day free trial.

Customer support is available via email and live chat, Monday through Friday, 9am to 6pm Eastern Time. Enterprise customers also get a dedicated account manager and 24/7 phone support.

Data is encrypted at rest and in transit, and Acme Widgets is SOC 2 Type II certified. Backups run every six hours and are retained for 30 days.
